Our Services

Sales, installation, repair and AMC for water purifiers — done right, the first time.

🛒

Purifier Sales

New RO, UV and UF water purifiers for homes, offices and shops. We help you pick the right model based on your water source (borewell/municipal), TDS level and household size.

🔧

Installation

Wall-mounted or under-sink fitting with proper piping, pressure checks, and a full demo of filter change and maintenance.

🛠️

Repair & Troubleshooting

Leakage, low water output, unusual taste/smell, motor or pump issues, membrane replacement — same-day visits wherever possible.

🧴

Filter & Membrane Replacement

Genuine sediment, carbon, RO membrane and UV lamp replacements — scheduled on time so water quality never drops.

Fixed-cost yearly plans covering scheduled visits, parts and priority repair support. See our AMC Plans.
